Delete your Manino account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Manino account (package com.fynlo.app, published by Fynlo Labs) and what happens to your data.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Manino and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm when prompted. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Your account and the data listed below are removed immediately on confirmation.

Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email maninosupport@gmail.com from the address linked to your Manino account with the subject Delete my account.

We verify the request against the account email and complete deletion within 30 days. You will receive confirmation once it is done.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataOutcome
Account identifier and email addressDeleted

Categories, tags and app preferencesDeleted
Subscription and purchase recordsKept up to 7 years, where tax and accounting law requires it
Anonymous, aggregated analytics and crash diagnostics that cannot be linked back to youKept indefinitely
Encrypted backups containing deleted recordsOverwritten within 30 days of deletion

Data stored on your device

Manino is local-first: most of your financial data lives on your phone. Deleting your account removes the server-side copy. To remove the local copy, uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Manino → Storage → Clear data.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual transactions, budgets, goals and lend or borrow records at any time from within the app. Deleted records are removed from our servers on the next sync.
