Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 May 2026 Last updated: 9 June 2026

Introduction

Fynlo ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal expense tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using Fynlo, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information we access

Fynlo accesses the following categories of information:

  • Expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and notes you add manually.
  • Contacts (with your permission) — when you use the Lend/Borrow feature, Fynlo requests access to your device contacts so you can associate a transaction with a person. Only the contact's name and phone number are read for this purpose.

How we use your contacts

Contact access is used solely to let you tag a person in a lending or borrowing record. We do not share your contacts with third parties or use them for any purpose other than displaying them within the app for your selection.

Contact associations are stored locally on your device. If you enable optional cloud sync, any associated contact names are encrypted in transit and at rest — only names linked to your transactions are synced, never your full address book.

Data storage

All data entered in Fynlo — including expense records, lending entries, and any contact associations — is stored locally on your device by default.

Fynlo also offers optional cloud sync. When you enable this feature, your data is securely uploaded to our cloud service so you can access it across multiple devices. Cloud sync is entirely opt-in; you can use the app without enabling it. You may also delete your cloud data at any time from within the app settings.

Cloud sync

When cloud sync is enabled:

  • Your expense records, categories, budgets, and lending entries are synced to a secure cloud service.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Your data is used solely to provide the sync service and is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • You can disable cloud sync and request deletion of your cloud data at any time via app settings or by contacting us.

Third-party sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. We do not use advertising networks or analytics services that collect personally identifiable information.

Data security

Local data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (screen lock, encryption). We recommend keeping your device secured with a PIN, password, or biometric lock.

When cloud sync is enabled, all data transmission is encrypted in transit using TLS, and data at rest is encrypted on our servers. Access is protected by your account credentials.

Permissions requested

  • READ_CONTACTS — To allow you to select a contact from your address book when adding a lend/borrow transaction. This is optional; you can use Fynlo without granting this permission.
  • INTERNET — Required for optional cloud sync. The app does not make any network requests if cloud sync is disabled.

Children's privacy

Fynlo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
